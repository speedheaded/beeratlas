How certain a number is

Six levels · one publishing rule

Every value on the site carries where it came from. The reader sees it, and so does a guide — the one person here who can catch us being wrong, and the one whose trust we cannot get back.

In running text

Kozel Černý is 10 °P and 3.8 %, among the weakest dark beers on a Prague tap. Bitterness sits around est. 20 IBU — low, though IBU is a poor guide to what you actually taste in a beer this sweet.

At Pivovarský klub it was 45 Kč (12 Sep 2026). At U Slovanské lípy the tap list needs re-checking — nobody has answered the phone since March.

Estimates never sit next to measured values without a mark. A reader who cannot tell them apart assumes the whole page is invented the moment they catch one.
Publishing rule

A beer page ships only if a venue is attached to it, and only if gravity and alcohol come from a source.

Everything else may be estimated, provided it is marked. Beers with no Prague venue live as a line in the brewery's range and get no page of their own — a page with nothing to click through to is exactly what the search engines are throwing away.
